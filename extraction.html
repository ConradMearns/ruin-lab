Load a .vsregion.json produced by the read-only Python extractor, or reopen an extraction project. Raw .vcdbs saves cannot be opened directly in this page.

01

Bound the buildMaterial clues suggest construction. Adjust the six bounds and terrain distances. Manual edits are locked; bounds remain a hard limit.

02

Make room for empty spaceMove the slice into a cellar. Paint intentional air or preview a bounded connected-air fill. Open or clipped fills can include outdoors: only accept after review.

03

Keep the full projectThe project preserves both voxel layers, original IDs, selected air, manual decisions, and opaque source chunks. It can contain private information. Save it locally.

04

Send a copy to the ruinerImport the simplified export in the Observatory. Terrain context is fixed; selected air is recorded as provenance. Coexisting fluids, chiseled geometry, inventories, entities, and decors are not simulated.

This is an extraction prototype—not a Vintage Story schematic writer or automatic player-build detector. The synthetic example's cellar is at X 15–24, Y 3–5, Z 15–24. No player-world data ships with this page.
